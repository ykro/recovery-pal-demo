# Eating and drinking after laparoscopic appendectomy

There is no strict diet after keyhole appendix removal. The goal is to keep fluids up, get your bowels moving, and give your body the fuel it needs to heal.

## First two or three days

- Start with fluids and light meals: soup, toast, yoghurt, crackers, rice, bananas.
- Eat small amounts often rather than big plates.
- Sip water regularly. Aim for pale-yellow urine.
- Avoid fizzy drinks for a few days, they add to the bloating.
- If you feel sick, stick to clear fluids and try again in an hour or two.

## From day three onwards

- Move back to your normal diet as your appetite returns.
- Include protein at each meal (eggs, fish, chicken, beans, dairy) to help healing.
- Fruit, vegetables and wholegrains help keep your bowels regular.
- Painkillers such as codeine can cause constipation, so extra fibre and water matter while you take them.

## Keeping bowels regular

- Drink 1.5 to 2 litres of fluid a day unless told otherwise.
- Gentle walking helps your gut wake up.
- Prunes, kiwi, oats and plenty of water are natural helpers.
- If you have not had a bowel movement by day three, tell me. Your team may suggest a mild laxative.

## Things to go easy on

- Very fatty, fried or spicy food in the first week, it can upset a sensitive tummy.
- Alcohol while taking painkillers, and in the first week generally.
- Large heavy meals late at night.

## If you feel sick or bloated

- Small sips, plain food, and rest.
- Peppermint tea can ease trapped wind.
- Tell me if vomiting continues or you cannot keep fluids down.
