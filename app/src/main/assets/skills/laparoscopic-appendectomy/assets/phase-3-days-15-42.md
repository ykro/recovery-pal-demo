# Phase 3 — Days 15 to 42: Back to full activity

The wounds on the outside are healed and the ones inside are catching up. This phase is about returning to your normal life at a sensible pace.

## What is normal

- Feeling almost back to your usual self, with stamina still building.
- Small pink or purple scars that will fade over several months.
- Occasional pulling or tightness around the scars when stretching.
- Numbness or tingling near a scar as the nerves recover. This can take months to settle.

## Activity allowed

- Full return to work, including physical jobs, usually from around week 3 to 4 if you feel ready.
- Gentle exercise such as brisk walking, cycling on the flat, swimming once the wounds are fully closed.
- Gradually reintroduce lifting and gym work from about week 4 to 6. Start light and stop if it hurts.
- Contact sport and heavy lifting: check with your surgeon, usually around week 6.

## Pain expectations

- Little to no pain day to day.
- Mild aching after a big day is fine, provided it settles overnight.
- No need for regular painkillers.

## What to check daily

- Scars: clean, closed, no new redness or swelling.
- Any lump or bulge near a scar, especially when coughing or standing.
- Bowels and appetite normal.
- Energy levels rising week on week.

## When to worry

- Any new pain in the tummy, particularly if it comes with fever.
- Temperature above 38 °C.
- A wound that reopens, becomes red, or leaks fluid.
- A bulge near a scar that does not go away when you lie down.
- Vomiting, or no bowel movement for several days with bloating.
- Persistent fatigue that is getting worse rather than better.

If any of these happen, tell me and we can contact your care team together.
