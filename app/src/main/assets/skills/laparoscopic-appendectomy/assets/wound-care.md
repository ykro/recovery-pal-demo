# Wound care after laparoscopic appendectomy

You have three small wounds, usually one in the belly button and two lower down. Each is about 1 cm and closed with stitches, glue or small strips.

## Dressings

- Keep the original dressings on for the first 48 hours unless they become wet or dirty.
- After that, you can leave the wounds uncovered or use a simple dry dressing if clothing rubs.
- If a dressing becomes soaked, replace it with a clean dry one and let me know.
- Do not pick at glue, strips or scabs. They will come off on their own in 7 to 14 days.

## Showering

- You can usually shower after 24 to 48 hours. Water running over the wounds is fine.
- Do not soak in a bath, hot tub or swimming pool until the wounds are fully healed, usually two weeks.
- Pat wounds dry gently with a clean towel. Do not rub.
- Avoid creams, lotions or powders on the wounds until they are closed.

## Stitches

- Most stitches used are dissolvable and disappear on their own.
- If you were told you have non-dissolvable stitches or clips, they are usually removed at 7 to 10 days by your GP practice or clinic.

## What healthy healing looks like

- A little pinkness right at the edges.
- Mild itching.
- A small amount of clear or slightly pink fluid in the first day or two.
- Bruising that fades over one to two weeks.

## Keep it clean

- Wash your hands before touching the area.
- Wear loose, soft clothing.
- The belly-button wound can trap moisture, so dry it carefully.

## Ask for help if

- Redness spreads outward from the wound.
- The wound feels hot, swollen or increasingly painful.
- Thick yellow, green or smelly fluid appears.
- A wound edge opens up.
- You have a fever alongside any wound change.
