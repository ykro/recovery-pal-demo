# Exercises with an ankle fracture in a cast or boot

Moving the parts that are not in the cast keeps blood flowing, reduces swelling, prevents clots, and stops the rest of your leg from getting stiff and weak. Do these little and often, several times a day.

## While in the cast or boot (all phases)

**Toe wiggles**
- Wiggle and spread your toes for 30 seconds, every hour while awake.
- Curl them down, then stretch them up.

**Knee bends**
- Lying or sitting, slide your heel towards your bottom to bend the knee, then straighten. Repeat 10 times.

**Straight leg raises**
- Lying on your back, tighten the thigh and lift the whole leg 20 cm off the bed. Hold 5 seconds, lower slowly. Repeat 10 times.

**Hip movements**
- Lying on your back, slide the leg out to the side and back. Repeat 10 times.
- Lying on your side, lift the top leg up and down. Repeat 10 times.

**Buttock squeezes**
- Squeeze the buttocks together, hold 5 seconds, relax. Repeat 10 times.

**Good leg and upper body**
- Keep the other leg and your arms active. Marching in a chair and light arm work help your fitness.

**Deep breathing**
- Five slow deep breaths every hour helps circulation.

## Weight bearing

- Follow your team's instructions exactly: non-weight bearing, partial, or full.
- If partial weight bearing has been allowed, practise standing with crutches and letting a small amount of weight through the heel of the cast or boot, as instructed.
- Never test the ankle before you have been told to.

## After the cast comes off (Phase 3)

Only once your team confirms the bone has healed.

**Ankle range of motion**
- Point the foot down and pull it up, 10 times.
- Turn the sole inwards and outwards, 10 times.
- Draw the alphabet in the air with your big toe.

**Calf stretch**
- Sit with a towel around the ball of the foot, gently pull towards you, hold 20 seconds.

**Strength and balance**
- Heel raises holding a chair, 10 times.
- Stand on the injured leg for 10 to 30 seconds with support nearby.

## Stop and report if

- Sharp pain, new swelling, or numbness.
- Any new calf pain.
