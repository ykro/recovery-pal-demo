# Exercises after ankle ORIF

Moving the parts that are free keeps blood flowing, reduces swelling and prevents clots. Do these little and often. Move the ankle itself only when your team says so.

## Phase 1 and while immobilised

**Toe wiggles**
- Wiggle and spread your toes for 30 seconds, every hour while awake.

**Knee bends**
- Lying or sitting, slide the heel towards your bottom to bend the knee, then straighten. Repeat 10 times.

**Straight leg raises**
- Lying on your back, tighten the thigh and lift the whole leg 20 cm. Hold 5 seconds, lower slowly. Repeat 10 times.

**Hip movements**
- Lying on your back, slide the leg out to the side and back, 10 times.
- Lying on your side, lift the top leg up and down, 10 times.

**Buttock squeezes**
- Squeeze, hold 5 seconds, relax. Repeat 10 times.

**Deep breathing**
- Five slow deep breaths every hour.

## Phase 2, if the boot is removable and your team allows

**Ankle range of motion**
- Point the foot down and pull it up, 10 times, gently, within comfort.
- Turn the sole inwards and outwards, 10 times.
- Draw the alphabet with your big toe.

## Weight bearing

- Follow your team's instructions exactly: non-weight bearing, touch, partial or full.
- When first allowed, practise standing with crutches and letting the instructed amount of weight through the boot.
- Progress from two crutches, to one, to none, as pain and confidence allow.

## Phase 3, once cleared for full weight bearing

**Calf stretch**
- Sit with a towel around the ball of the foot and gently pull towards you, hold 20 seconds, 3 times.

**Heel raises**
- Holding a chair, rise onto your toes and lower slowly, 10 times.

**Balance**
- Stand on the operated leg for 10 to 30 seconds with support nearby, 3 times.

**Step-ups**
- Step up onto a low step with the operated leg, step down, 10 times.

## Stop and report if

- Sharp pain over the metalwork, new swelling, or numbness.
- Any new calf pain.
